Frequently Asked Questions

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

You can view invoices, update your payment method, or cancel anytime from your account management page. Enter the email you used at checkout and we'll send you a link.

What devices does CamGrader support?

CamGrader is available on iOS (iPhone, iOS 16 and later) and Android. Check the App Store or Google Play for the most current device requirements.

What answer sheet formats are supported?

CamGrader supports 25-question, 50-question, and 100-question bubble sheets (A–D format). Sheets are available as PDF and JPEG in multiple print darkness levels so they work reliably on any printer.

Do I have to use the matching sheet size for my test?

No. You can use any sheet format for any test length. For example, a 20-question test can use the 25-Q sheet. Students simply leave the extra bubbles blank and CamGrader grades only the questions on the answer key.

Is student data stored securely?

Yes. Student IDs, scores, and test results are stored in an encrypted local database on your device and are never shared with third parties. Deleting the app removes all data.

Can I use CamGrader for standardized tests?

CamGrader is designed for classroom tests and teacher-created assessments. For state-mandated standardized tests, please follow your district's approved procedures.

Can I use CamGrader without internet?

Yes. CamGrader is fully offline. All grading happens on your device. No internet is required to scan, grade, or view results. An internet connection is only used to download answer sheet templates or install app updates.

Why are some bubbles not being detected?

Make sure you're using the correct answer sheet format for your test (25-Q, 50-Q, or 100-Q). Try a lighter print darkness if bubbles are too dark, or a darker print if they're too light. Hold the sheet flat, well-lit, and parallel to the screen. Download sheet templates from the Sheets tab inside the app.

The app says "MULTIPLE" for a question. What does that mean?

This means the scanner detected more than one filled bubble for that question. Ask the student to re-check their sheet. If the sheet was clean but the error persists, try scanning at better lighting or with a lighter-darkness sheet.

How do I export grades to my gradebook?

Open the test, tap the Share / Export button, and choose "Export as CSV." The file includes each student's ID and score and can be opened in Excel, Google Sheets, or any gradebook that accepts CSV.
