Export student results

Help Center · Results & Export · Export Student Results

For each student, you can export a PDF that includes their scanned answer sheet thumbnail and a complete per-question breakdown. Useful for parent conferences, student feedback, or your own records.

1. Open the test and tap a student

From the home screen, tap the test, then tap any student's row in the results list to open their individual result.

2. Review the result, then tap Export PDF

The result screen shows the student's score, their scanned answer sheet, and the Question Breakdown legend - once it looks right, tap Export PDF right below the sheet.

3. Choose where to send it

Tapping Export PDF opens your device's share sheet with the file ready to go - pick a contact or app, save it straight to Drive, or print it directly. No internet connection is needed to open the PDF itself.

4. What's in the PDF

Choosing an app like WhatsApp shows a preview before you send - exactly what's in the PDF: the test name, course, sheet format, student ID and score, a thumbnail of their scanned sheet, and a full question-by-question table of their answer against the key.
