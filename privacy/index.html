Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 14, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 17, 2026

CamGrader is a grading app built for teachers. This policy explains what data the app collects, where it goes, and how it is handled.

1. Two Ways to Use CamGrader

Guest users do not create an account. All classroom data stays on your device in a local database. Nothing is transmitted to our servers.

Signed-in users create an account with their email address, which lets us track your monthly scan usage against your account instead of your device. Paid subscribers additionally get cloud backup, which stores your data on Supabase servers so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or switch devices. Before cloud backup is enabled, you are shown a plain-language consent screen explaining exactly what gets backed up and where it goes.

2. Data You Enter

When you use CamGrader you create tests, scan answer sheets, and the app records:

  • Test names, question counts, and answer keys
  • Student ID numbers detected from scanned answer sheets
  • Per-question grading results and scores
  • Scan thumbnails saved to your device

For guest users, all of this stays on your device. Deleting the app removes it permanently.

For signed-in users, this data is also backed up to Supabase after you give consent. Camera frames are processed on-device and are never transmitted externally.

3. Authentication

When you sign in, you provide your email address. CamGrader uses Supabase Auth to manage your account. A one-time sign-in code is sent to your email via Resend, an email delivery service. Resend receives your email address solely to deliver this code. No password is ever created or stored. Your email address is stored in Supabase and is used to identify your account and restore your backed-up data.

4. Cloud Backup (Paid Subscribers)

Cloud backup and sync are available to paid subscribers only. Signing in without a subscription lets us track your scan usage, but does not back up your test, student, or result data to the cloud - see Section 2 of our Terms of Service for the exact limits at each tier. With your consent, once you subscribe, CamGrader backs up the following to Supabase:

  • Tests and answer keys
  • Student ID numbers and scan results
  • Scan images (stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket)

Cloud backup happens automatically in the background when a connection is available, and can also be triggered manually from Settings using "Sync All Now." Your device remains the source of truth. Backed-up data is stored on Supabase infrastructure hosted in South Korea and is governed by Supabase's Privacy Policy. See Section 13 for a full list of the service providers CamGrader uses and where each stores data.

5. Crash Reporting

CamGrader uses Sentry for crash reporting and performance monitoring. When the app encounters an unexpected error, Sentry automatically captures a report containing:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • A technical stack trace describing where the error occurred

Crash reports never include classroom data, student IDs, test content, or scores. They are used solely to identify and fix bugs. Sentry data is stored in the European Union and processed in accordance with Sentry's Privacy Policy.

CamGrader also uses Sentry's Session Replay feature, which can record a video-like reconstruction of on-screen activity for a small sample of app sessions (5%) and a larger sample of sessions where an error occurs (75%), to help us understand and fix bugs that are hard to reproduce from a stack trace alone. All text and images on screen are masked in these recordings, so classroom data, student IDs, test content, and scores are never visible in a Session Replay recording. Session Replay data is stored and governed the same way as the crash reports described above.

6. Student Data

CamGrader reads numeric student ID numbers from printed bubble grids on answer sheets. No student names, photos, or government-issued IDs are collected. As the teacher using the app, you are responsible for ensuring your use of student data complies with applicable regulations in your jurisdiction, including FERPA in the United States.

For signed-in users, student ID numbers and associated scores are included in the cloud backup described in Section 4. They are stored on your account only and are never shared with any third party.

7. Permissions

CamGrader requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera: required to scan printed answer sheets. Camera frames are processed on-device and never transmitted externally.
  • Local file system access: required to save scan thumbnails and exported files to your device.
  • Network access (signed-in users only): required to sync with Supabase and to receive sign-in codes via email.

8. Data Export

CamGrader includes an Export feature that lets you save a backup of your tests and results as a file, shared through your device's native share sheet. You choose where it goes. We do not receive a copy of any exported file.

9. Deleting Your Data

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Individual tests or students: swipe to delete in the app. Local and cloud data are removed immediately.
  • All data (Clear All Data): available in Settings. Removes all tests and students from your device and from Supabase.
  • Your account (Delete Account): available in Settings. Permanently removes all your data from Supabase, including tests, students, scan results, scan images, and your account record, and signs you out. Supabase automated backups may retain the data briefly before it is fully purged.

To request deletion by email, contact hello@camgrader.com.

10. Data Retention

Data for active accounts is retained as long as the account exists. If you delete your account, your data is permanently removed from Supabase. Accounts that have been inactive for 24 months are notified by email and given 30 days to sign in before data is deleted.

11. Children's Privacy

CamGrader is intended for use by teachers and educational professionals. The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app adds new features. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

13. Subprocessors

CamGrader uses the following third-party service providers ("subprocessors") to operate the app. Each is bound by a Data Processing Agreement covering how it handles any personal data it processes on our behalf.

  • Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage for signed-in users) — data hosted in South Korea. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe (payment processing for subscriptions, including your billing address, which is used to calculate any applicable sales tax or VAT), contracted through Stripe Payments Europe, Limited. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Resend (delivers your one-time sign-in code by email) — data hosted in the United States. See Resend's Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry (crash and error reporting, described in Section 5) — data hosted in the European Union. See Sentry's Privacy Policy.
  • Mixpanel (product analytics; for signed-in users, this includes your account email address so usage can be tied to your account) — data hosted in the United States. See Mixpanel's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase (crash-adjacent analytics and basic usage tracking; does not collect classroom data) — hosted on Google's standard infrastructure. See Firebase's Privacy and Security page.
  • Google Tag Manager (loads analytics and, if added in the future, advertising tags — currently only Google Analytics 4) — only loads after you accept cookies on the banner shown on your first visit; your choice is remembered in your browser so you won't be asked again. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare (proxies and rate-limits API requests to protect against abuse and outages) — see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy and Data Processing Addendum.
  • Expo (delivers app updates directly to your device without requiring an App Store or Play Store update; these requests include your device's IP address and operating system version, but no persistent device identifier) — see Expo's Privacy Policy.

We review this list periodically and will update it if a subprocessor is added, removed, or changes where it stores data.

14. Your US Privacy Rights (California and Other States)

If you are a California resident, or a resident of another US state with a similar privacy law, you have rights over the personal information CamGrader holds about you: the right to know what's collected, the right to delete it (see Section 9), and the right to correct inaccurate information. To exercise any of these rights, contact hello@camgrader.com.

We do not sell or share your personal information (as those terms are defined under California and similar state laws) to third parties for money or for their own advertising purposes. The subprocessors listed in Section 13 process data only on our instructions, under a Data Processing Agreement, to operate CamGrader — not to use it for their own purposes. Because we don't sell or share personal information, there's nothing for a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" request to opt out of, but we still honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal as an opt-out preference where applicable.

We do not use sensitive personal information (such as precise geolocation, or the contents of your communications) for any purpose beyond providing the app's core functionality, so there is nothing to limit under a "Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information" request.

We will not deny you service, charge you a different price, or provide a different level of quality because you exercised any of these rights.

15. Contact Form and Website Enquiries

If you submit our contact form, we collect the name, email address, topic, and message you provide. For institutional quote requests, this may also include your school or district name, location, and estimated number of teachers and students, if you choose to include them. Submissions are stored in our database and trigger a notification email to our team; you'll also receive an automatic acknowledgement email confirming we received your message. We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, and it is not linked to any CamGrader app account unless you sign in separately. To request deletion of a submission, email hello@camgrader.com.
