Simple by design

Three steps. That's it.

No training required. If you can take a photo, you can use CamGrader.

1
Scan the answer key

Generate a bubble-sheet answer key in the app (or use the web tool), hold it up to your phone's camera, and CamGrader reads it instantly.

2
Scan student sheets

Students fill in their bubble sheets on paper. You scan each one and CamGrader detects the student ID and scores every question automatically.

3
Review & export

See scores, per question breakdowns, and class statistics instantly. Export results as CSV to paste into any gradebook.

No Wi-Fi is required to scan and grade. All grading and scoring happens on your device. An internet connection is only used for things like downloading answer sheet templates or installing app updates.
CamGrader supports 25-question, 50-question, and 100-question bubble sheets (A–D format). Sheets are available as PDF and JPEG in multiple print darkness levels so they work reliably on any printer.
