4. Cloud Backup, Sync, and Data Restore

Cloud backup and sync are available to paid subscribers only. If you are signed in but not subscribed, your scan usage is tracked on our servers, but your test, student, and result data is not backed up to the cloud and cannot be restored if lost from your device.

When you sign in, whether on a new device, a device switching from a different account under the one device policy in Section 3, or a device that already has your account's data, CamGrader checks for and pulls down any data that exists in our cloud backup but not yet on that device. The same check happens automatically in the background and whenever you tap "Sync Now" in Settings, which checks for differences in both directions and pulls what's missing locally, then pushes what's missing in the cloud. Your device remains the primary copy of your data: syncing happens at these points (sign-in, background intervals, and manual sync) rather than continuously and instantly the moment a change is made on another device. If a different account's data is already present on a device, you will be offered the option to export that data before it is removed from the device (this removal is local only and does not affect that account's own cloud backup), and your data is then restored automatically.

If your device is offline when you delete a test, student, or result, the deletion is applied to your local data immediately and to your cloud backup automatically the next time your device reconnects. Until that sync completes, the deleted data may still exist in our systems, though it remains private to your account in the meantime.
